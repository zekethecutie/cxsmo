# Instagram Portfolio Reference Findings

Source reviewed: https://www.instagram.com/p/DbTcV9mDEDk/

## What the post emphasizes

The carousel frames its message around a fast, binary recruitment decision: **“Portfolios they skip” versus “Portfolios they hire.”** It then shifts to a practical claim that recruiters make a rapid decision and encourages designers to invest in the sections recruiters actually scan. The post’s stated focus includes the opening work, the framing of case studies, and the about page.

## Transferable principles for C✦SMO

1. **Lead with the proof, not the premise.** C✦SMO should open with a tightly framed, obvious example of the product and interaction craft before asking a reviewer to explore its visual system.
2. **Make the creator role explicit.** The current zxke credit can evolve from footer-only attribution into a concise design-and-build proposition at the first decisive scroll point.
3. **Make each major route read like a case-study chapter.** The strongest public sections should state their problem, creative direction, and implemented interaction in compact factual language.
4. **Show the craft evidence.** Before/after or “design decision” callouts would better explain why C✦SMO uses local commerce simulation, full-cast Fit Edits, and direct public media.
5. **Use a clear close.** The root landing should give prospective clients a direct, low-friction route to email or GitHub after the work has demonstrated its capability.

## Additional carousel observations

The reference contrasts a generic self-description with a focused role-plus-impact statement, then contrasts generic project labels with outcome-led case-study titles. C✦SMO should adopt the same principle without using unverified business metrics: identify the exact design problem and the implemented system rather than naming a page merely as an ecommerce concept.

Suggested original positioning: **“zxke designs motion-led commerce systems for fashion brands: C✦SMO pairs editorial product storytelling with responsive, browser-local shopping flows.”**

## Boundary

These are original portfolio-communication recommendations. They do not reproduce the post’s imagery, copy, slide composition, or claimed research findings.
