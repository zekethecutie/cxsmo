# C✦SMO Poster Hero Hierarchy Audit

The active poster hero uses one primary visual object: the published transparent campaign layer at `.poster-hero__object-layer--campaign`. It is the sole campaign `<img>` in the hero markup; the surrounding backword, frame, gradient star, type, card, barcode, and scroll cue are interface layers rather than competing image subjects.

The depth system is scroll-linked and deliberately differentiated. The campaign object moves from `0%` to `-14%` and eases from `-4deg` to `-1deg`; the type moves to `18%`; the background C✦SMO word moves to `48%`; and the barcode moves to `44%`. The restored composition also keeps the campaign image’s controlled black depth shadow and the type’s gradient emphasis without changing its user-approved vertical campaign placement.

The shared hero layer contract has been reviewed in dark and light appearances at desktop and mobile widths. The front-to-back repair remains intact: campaign at 300, static study card at 350, menu at 260, and custom cursor above page content.
