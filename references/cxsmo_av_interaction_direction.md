# C✦SMO Audiovisual Interaction Direction

## Experience principle

Sound and motion should behave as **optional editorial punctuation**, not as decoration on every interface event. The site must never autoplay audio. After a visitor explicitly enables sound, short effects confirm intentional actions, while the guided showcase carries the richer mograph cadence. `prefers-reduced-motion` and the local “sound off” setting disable nonessential choreography and all feedback audio.

## Sound map

| Moment | Asset | Intended treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Sound enabled | `mixkit-opening-software-interface-2578` | One quiet confirmation after the visitor turns sound on. |
| Standard button / menu selection | `mixkit-interface-device-click-2577` | Throttled, low-volume confirmation for discrete primary clicks only. |
| Save, add-to-bag, or local preference confirmation | `mixkit-quick-positive-video-game-notification-interface-265` | Positive state change, never used for a fictional transaction. |
| Theme shift | `mixkit-magic-sparkle-whoosh-2350` | Single transition accent, paired with the visual eclipse. |
| Product media-stage switch | `mixkit-camera-digital-shutter-1432` | Used only on Object/Worn view changes. |
| Guided-showcase launch | `mixkit-game-bonus-reached-2065` | The requested “game bonus” brand-build cue, played only after the visitor starts the showcase. |
| Guided-showcase chapter transition | `mixkit-explainer-video-pops-whoosh-light-pop-3005` | A restrained section-to-section cue. |
| Guided-showcase completion | `mixkit-quick-win-video-game-notification-269` | A single closing reward sound when the visitor reaches the final chapter. |

## Appearance transition

The previous pixel-field wash is replaced by a **signal eclipse**. A four-point star ignites from the appearance button’s corner, expands into a radial chrome-red aperture, and resolves into the destination palette. The spatial effect is composed only from opacity, transforms, masks, and gradients. It lasts roughly half a second, has no flashing strobe, and becomes an instant change for reduced-motion visitors.

## Guided showcase

The footer launches a user-controlled “Play C✦SMO” overlay. It is not an unattended auto-scroll or fake cursor. The visitor explicitly starts it, can pause, skip chapters, mute sound, or exit with Escape. Chapters use a vertically sequenced stage inside the overlay rather than issuing browser scroll commands:

| Chapter | Visual message | Demonstrated platform surface |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **C✦SMO / SIGNAL IN** | Brand mark construction, black-red-bone system, and sound opt-in. |
| 02 | **OBJECTS, NOT TEMPLATES** | Poster hero, campaign layers, product treatment, and hero hierarchy. |
| 03 | **COMMERCE, SIMULATED HONESTLY** | Bag, local profile, locale-aware price display, and non-payment checkout boundary. |
| 04 | **OPERATIONS, EDITABLE** | Studio content control, media library, and portfolio-safe operations. |
| 05 | **BUILT TO BE FELT** | Appearance transition, motion system, accessibility controls, and clear exit. |

Each chapter uses mograph-inspired typography, an animated progress rail, and a contextual tooltip. It references the actual route it is explaining without submitting forms, adding products, changing preferences, or impersonating a shopper.

## Canvas media boundary

The user’s canvas preview establishes that manually background-removed C✦SMO product and campaign assets exist. The raw exported PNG files have not yet appeared in the sandbox’s static-asset workspace, so current public code must continue to use registered assets until the corresponding exported files are available for import.
