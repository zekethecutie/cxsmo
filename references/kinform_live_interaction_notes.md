# KINFORM Live Interaction Verification

The live KINFORM portfolio storefront was opened in the browser after the Ruixen and 21st.dev-inspired refinements.

The hero displayed the generated Line Tee against the WebGL material field. The selected-product action opened the Line Tee detail sheet with the garment visual, fictional price, sizing controls, material notes, add-to-bag action, and a clear portfolio-only payment disclaimer.

Adding the product updated the header bag count from zero to one and opened the bag panel. The bag panel showed the selected Line Tee, colour, fictional price, and another explicit statement that no live payment or shipping applies. This verifies the core demo commerce flow without presenting the fictional products as purchasable goods.
