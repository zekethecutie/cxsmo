# C✦SMO Poster System

## Intent

C✦SMO will read as a **future-retro fashion transmission**, not as a reproduction of any reference poster. The system combines photographic streetwear framing with black, blood red, bone, and polished-silver surfaces. Large condensed type establishes rhythm; quieter mono labels carry the commerce information; the four-point star remains the one unmistakable C✦SMO sign.

## Composition grammar

Each feature surface uses three depth planes. A soft or blurred background establishes movement, one crisp model or product plane provides focus, and a foreground type, star, barcode, or chrome-frame layer creates the poster interruption. The strongest red appears as a large field or type block, never as a decorative scatter. Long stacked text, split portrait crops, and oversized numerals create motion without reducing product clarity.

## Motion grammar

Motion is intentionally cinematic rather than constant. Hero layers respond to pointer position with restrained parallax. Text reveals use translation and opacity only, while scroll-led panels pin or pass through one another to create a magazine-sequence effect. Buttons, swatches, saves, filters, and cards use short responsive feedback. All nonessential movement must collapse under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Commerce grammar

Product price, category, fit, and size action remain immediately visible even inside expressive campaigns. Every portfolio-safe boundary is direct: no payment, no fabricated social proof, no fake order history, and no invented fulfilment claims. The public footer credits **Developed by zxke**.
