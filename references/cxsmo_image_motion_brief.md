# C✦SMO Image and Motion Brief

## Visual frame

The next C✦SMO image set is **original editorial streetwear**: dynamic full-body figures, cropped at varying distances, dressed in oversized denim, fitted graphics, technical outerwear, and chrome accessories. Each image will have a deliberate role rather than acting as generic wallpaper. The hero uses one decisive motion frame; lookbook panels use three independent fit narratives; product views use isolated garment or object renders on transparent backgrounds; and the carousel uses visual contrasts between silhouette, pose, and crop.

## Poster treatment

The black, red, and bone system remains constant. Photography will be layered with controlled blur, sharp foreground cut-outs, oversized type, code labels, star flashes, and a small amount of chromed material detail. The supplied examples inform the energy, density, collage rhythm, and editorial confidence only; C✦SMO will not reproduce their people, brands, text, composition, or exact styling.

## Motion and interaction

The fit carousel advances through momentum-based horizontal movement and clear previous/next controls. Cards react with depth on hover, while CTA controls use quick underline, offset, and press feedback. Image and copy layers will enter with translation and opacity rather than layout-shifting animation. The system retains a static, legible experience when reduced motion is preferred.

## Community boundary

The customer-feedback module is a design-ready, **empty verified-feedback state**. It may invite future verified submissions but must not show invented names, ratings, testimonials, comments, orders, or usage claims.
