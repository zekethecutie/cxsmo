# C✦SMO Next Asset Session

## Purpose

Create an **original visual library** that replaces repeated campaign crops with distinct product, fit, and hero compositions. The imagery must make C✦SMO feel more photogenic, dynamic, witty, and genuinely fashion-editorial while preserving the portfolio’s fictional boundary.

## Required image set

| Asset | Framing | Use in C✦SMO | Non-negotiable constraint |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hero v2 | Full-body model, 16:9, left-side copy safe area | Poster landing hero | Dramatic active pose; visual focal point differs from current group campaign |
| Denim cutout | Single garment, transparent 3:4 PNG | Product cards and product stage | Clean alpha; no body, background, label, or copied branding |
| Graphic-tee cutout | Single garment, transparent 3:4 PNG | Product cards and product stage | Clear tee silhouette and small original graphic treatment |
| Moto-shell cutout | Single garment, transparent 3:4 PNG | Product cards and product stage | Chrome hardware and cropped technical shape rendered credibly |
| Fit 01 | Full-body, 4:5 | Fit carousel | Low-angle motion; baggy denim plus close-fitting layer |
| Fit 02 | Full-body, 4:5 | Fit carousel | Dynamic turn or walking pose; different model and silhouette from Fit 01 |
| Fit 03 | Full-body, 4:5 | Fit carousel | Outerwear-led form; editorial flash and red-black set accent |
| Object flat-lay | 4:5 | Category and inventory visual treatment | Original accessories and garment overlap, designed for annotation overlays |

## Art direction

The photos should use the user-supplied examples only as a reference for **energy**: dynamic posing, extreme scale shifts, short-depth-of-field blur, dense editorial type-safe negative space, art-directed red accents, and a confident fashion-poster silhouette. They must not reuse recognisable people, clothing, branding, text, or composition from those references. Use young-adult fictional models, original wardrobe styling, mixed hard-flash and soft studio light, black/red/bone sets, and visible full-body proportions.

## Asset acceptance check

Every cutout needs a genuine transparent alpha channel, complete edges, no fringe, no accidental lettering, and no visible background. Editorial images need a readable focal figure, a distinct pose, no copied logos, no watermark, and enough unoccupied space for the C✦SMO interface to place live typography over the image.
