# CXS✦MO Design Brief

## Brand premise

**CXS✦MO** is an explicitly fictional, high-energy fashion and lifestyle concept. The name is spoken as “Cosmo,” while its X is represented by a compact four-point star. It replaces the public-facing KINFORM treatment in this portfolio demonstration with a materially different proposition: collectible Y2K pop, styled-world editorial imagery, and wearable street silhouettes.

## Direction drawn from the supplied references

The new system will pair generous, low-slung and puddled denim with fitted graphic layers, baseball and moto references, stripe tailoring, leather-effect outerwear, silver hardware, utility straps, small camera and charm accessories, and contrast footwear. The editorial language is photo-first: flash-lit, candid, high-saturation campaign frames alongside clean product cutouts and collage-like “fit check” compositions.

The palette is optic white, ink black, faded denim, chrome, electric blue, cherry red, and acid-lime accents. The layout should retain enough negative space to present the assortment as premium rather than chaotic. Product names, categories, and merchandising stories are fictional, and no customer reviews, transactions, or fulfilment events will be represented as real.

## Experience architecture

The public journey will foreground categories, styled edits, product discovery, size and fit guidance, saved items, bag, and shipping-information states. The operations journey will make category management, product and variant editing, inventory movements, orders, receipts, POS, shipping, customer records, reporting, and content publishing legible as a portfolio demonstration—without presenting the experience as an active payment or fulfilment system.
