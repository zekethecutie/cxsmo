# KNIALL Production Art Direction

KNIALL should not copy a component gallery or a sports campaign page. The working reference blend is a calm product story with one cinematic hero, a strict clothing-first merchandising path, and polished utility surfaces.

The Void Energy live site demonstrates a restrained navigation shell, a highly focused hero, a strong visual wordmark, and an immersive surface that does not need many independent blocks. The KNIALL translation is not a dark clone. It is a light, warm, spatially calm product stage with a single atmospheric scene and a clear route to buy.

The Webflow Avolin fashion commerce reference reinforces the operational layer that must sit beneath the art direction: campaign landing surfaces, collection browsing, a product grid, individual product pages with image gallery and size selection, bag, checkout, mobile response, and information architecture that supports a real store. KNIALL should use a custom art direction on top of this functional foundation.

Design rules for the rebuild:

1. Use one modern system sans for interface and body text, with normal rather than thin weights. A compact Apple-like interface feel comes from clear spacing, 14 to 16 pixel body text, strong weight contrast, and high quality controls, not tiny mono labels.
2. Use one refined condensed display face only for a limited number of campaign moments. Do not repeat huge display typography across every section.
3. Keep the main canvas warm off-white, pale stone, and soft graphite. KNIALL gold appears as an accent, a logo signal, and an active control. It should not tint every surface.
4. The hero uses a light atmospheric backdrop, product photo or garment composition, gentle depth, and one clear action. It should avoid product codes, dense labels, and equipment language.
5. Use 21st.dev principles as interaction vocabulary: soft button states, glassy but restrained panels, clean drawers, surface elevation, precise animation, and fewer but better effects.
6. Use one Spline or WebGL material study only after the product data and art direction are stable. It must have a static fallback and must enhance the clothing story, such as a fabric ripple, KNIALL mark sculpture, or garment form.
