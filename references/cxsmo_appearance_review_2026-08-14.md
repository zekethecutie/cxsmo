# C✦SMO Appearance Review — 14 August 2026

Explicit light and dark route previews were captured for the poster landing page, catalogue, Information Desk, and studio overview at desktop and mobile widths. The primary C✦SMO public surfaces preserve legible headline, support-copy, category, control, and disclosure contrast in both modes. The studio intentionally retains its own black-red operational palette in either public appearance mode; it is not a theme failure.

| Surface | Appearance result | Mobile result |
|---|---|---|
| Poster landing | The campaign composition remains deliberately dark while the header adapts between ink and bone. The headline, action buttons, campaign tag, and lower metadata remain distinct. | The group composition, copy, and campaign card remain legible without horizontal clipping. |
| Catalogue | Ink-on-bone and bone-on-ink type retain their hierarchy; category labels, filter controls, and product cards remain readable. | The horizontal category filter presents its intentional clipped/faded continuation affordance. |
| Information Desk | Open accordions preserve readable labels, answer copy, borders, and close markers in both modes. | The open state and subsequent topics remain visible and separated. |
| Studio overview | The studio retains its deliberate black-red control-room palette across appearance modes. | The compact dashboard keeps its disclosure strip, hero copy, metrics, and navigation trigger legible. |

The audit found one concrete navigation issue: at the public mobile breakpoint the `Navigate` disclosure was visually hidden rather than replaced with an accessible compact trigger. This was corrected with a compact mobile trigger that remains visible beside the logo, appearance control, sound control, and bag in both reviewed modes. This is a route-discovery correction rather than a color-contrast issue.
