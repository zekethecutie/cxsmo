# KINFORM Expansion Architecture

## Purpose

KINFORM remains a fictional fashion-commerce flagship for portfolio presentation. The expanded experience demonstrates how a mature apparel storefront can move from discovery to product evaluation, bag management, support, and owner operations without presenting fictional data as a live business result.

## Public Route Map

| Route | Role | Key interactions |
|---|---|---|
| `kinform` | Editorial storefront and collection entry | Hero product study, material backdrop, product selection, bag entry, journal teaser |
| `kinform collection` | Browseable collection index | Product grid, composition-first filters, quiet sorting, collection story, saved-object affordance |
| `kinform product` | Product evaluation page | Gallery, fabric specification, sizing, care, availability, bag action, verified-feedback empty state |
| `kinform journal` | Editorial and campaign index | Campaign stories, field notes, product-context links |
| `kinform journal passage` | Long-form editorial page | Campaign visual, readable narrative, related garments |
| `kinform support` | Customer-support information | Delivery guidance, returns policy placeholder, sizing support, contact handoff |
| `kinform account` | Customer-account experience placeholder | Saved objects, order-state placeholder, sign-in handoff clearly marked as portfolio-only |
| `kinform bag` | Bag-focused edit state | Selected objects, quantity-ready layout, fictional checkout entry |
| `kinform admin` | Owner-facing portfolio administration demonstration | Catalogue operations, stock posture, campaign planning, product media, customer-service queues |

## Feedback and Social Proof Boundary

The review system is deliberately **data-ready, not fabricated**. The product page will include an elegant verified-feedback section with a zero-review state explaining that verified purchase feedback appears only after real orders. It will not show invented star averages, review counts, customer comments, ratings, or testimonials.

The administration demonstration will show the review workflow as a disabled or empty operational state. It may describe moderation capability, photo-review collection, verification status, and helpful-vote design, but it will not seed fictional customer content or aggregate scores.

## Administration Demonstration

| Module | Portfolio-safe demonstration data | Future live data source |
|---|---|---|
| Catalogue | KINFORM object names, fictional prices, visual completeness, draft status | Shopify catalogue and product media |
| Inventory posture | Fictional sample quantities clearly labelled as portfolio data | Shopify inventory levels |
| Campaign planning | Original KINFORM campaign assets and editorial schedule | Owner-managed campaign calendar |
| Product media | Original apparel renders and campaign assets | Approved product photography and media library |
| Customer service | Empty inbox state and workflow controls | Real support system or Shopify customer records |
| Verified feedback | Zero-review state and moderation model | Verified orders and consented customer feedback |

## Design Principles

The public experience will remain spacious, considered, and product-led. Asceternity-style interaction patterns will be used selectively for meaningful moments such as focus-aware action buttons, soft spotlight surfaces, and depth cues. Ruixen-style navigation treatment will continue as a tactile interface layer rather than becoming a visual effect on every element.

The administration route will be calmer and more operational: clear hierarchy, measurable product readiness, image-led merchandising tools, and empty states that make the future workflow understandable. It will not masquerade as KNIALL’s real back office or claim access to business data.
